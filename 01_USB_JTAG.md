# For now to get first contact: Enable USB-Serial/JTAG

Easy to open, just start with something thin as in the picture to go inside, no glue, no screws.
R4 and R5, are not populated, they are in the line of USB D+/D- <=> ESP 18/19 GPIO.
For your comfort there are also small holes, a thin wire to put through as 0 Ohm will do the job 
=> USB-Serial/JTAG ✅ 

ℹ️ Spoiler: I also suspect OTA capabilities, but I haven't figuered out the WPA2 password yet...

While you are there, you can also add the 8 pin header, that will give you debug output from UART_1.

There is now nothing in the way 🛣️ to read complete flash or provide your own code.

Thats all folks, have phun 😛
